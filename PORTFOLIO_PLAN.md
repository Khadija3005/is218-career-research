# Portfolio Plan — Career Research

IS218-003 · Review 1 · November 12, 2026

## Research question and audience
How do customer-facing AI engineering and business systems implementation differ, and what evidence helps an internship recruiter assess preparation?

Working public direction: business systems implementation with AI; alternative: customer-facing AI engineering. This is a hypothesis to review, not a final career commitment. Audience: internship recruiters and hiring managers. Full personal comparison/reflection and personal eligibility circumstances remain private in Canvas.

## Employer evidence
The three original descriptions, source dates, responsibilities, requirements, short supporting excerpts and limitations are in [the browsable site](https://khadija3005.github.io/is218-career-research/#sources). Sources checked November 12, 2026. All are experienced roles. FDE source has inconsistent experience thresholds; clarification remains needed.

## Two design directions
A — Clear systems: ink/teal/warm white, serif headings, evidence tables and direct navigation. Selected for readable evidence and fast scanning. Tradeoff: can feel less personal.
B — Creative project journal: warm brown/peach, image-led tiles and narrative timeline. Alternative for an approachable project story. Tradeoff: longer route to checks.

## Five stories
1. Recruiter identifies professional direction; partner explains it after reading the introduction.
2. Reviewer finds and opens original employer sources and checked dates.
3. Hiring manager distinguishes actual contribution and checks from planned work.
4. Reader follows cross-links between career and programming research.
5. Phone/keyboard user reads content and navigates without confusion.
